# Duco

> Duco is an AI-powered operational data automation platform. It helps financial institutions normalize, validate and reconcile any data in the cloud — cutting the time spent on manual data work, reducing operational risk and accelerating processes across the front, middle and back office.

Duco (du.co) provides no-code data reconciliation, data preparation, intelligent document processing (Adaptive IDP) and agentic AI automation for capital markets firms, asset managers, brokers, asset servicers and market infrastructure. The platform ingests data from any source — including unstructured documents — then normalizes, validates and reconciles it, with full audit and control.

## Product
- [Duco Platform](https://du.co/product/duco-platform/): Normalize, validate and reconcile any data in the cloud.
- [Reconciliation](https://du.co/product/reconciliation/): Cloud-based, no-code reconciliation that ingests and normalizes data from multiple sources.
- [Transform & Prepare](https://du.co/product/data-prep/): No-code tool to normalize, transform and enrich operational data.
- [Extract & Load](https://du.co/product/extract-load/): Ingest data from any source, including unstructured documents.
- [Adaptive IDP](https://du.co/product/duco-platform/adaptive-idp/): Intelligent document processing that extracts unstructured data hidden in emails, PDFs and documents.
- [Agentic Workspace](https://du.co/agentic-workspace/): Agentic AI workspace for operational data automation.
- [Middle Office Transformation](https://du.co/middle-office-transformation/): Transform middle-office operations with automation.
